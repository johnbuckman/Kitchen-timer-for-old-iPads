## Description (max 4000)
Decent Timer puts four large, tap-friendly timers on a single screen — two that count up and two that count down — so you can track several things at once without juggling apps.

Built for the kitchen counter:
• Big, glanceable numbers and large buttons you can hit with messy hands.
• A clock and the day of the week right at the top.
• Set any duration with – / + (hold to change fast), or tap a timer to type it on a full-screen keypad. The keypad is smart: enter “1.5h”, “90s”, “1:30”, or just “5”.
• Count-downs keep going past zero into overtime, with a gentle repeating ding and a flashing screen, so you know how long ago it finished.
• While a timer runs it shows when it started and its estimated finish time.
• After a while idle it switches to a tidy full-screen clock (with the Decent Espresso logo) that drifts slightly to avoid burn-in — so it doubles as a counter-top clock.
• Works in portrait and landscape.

No sign-in. No ads. No tracking. Nothing is collected.

Made by Decent Espresso.
