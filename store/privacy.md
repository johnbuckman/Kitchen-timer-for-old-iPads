# App Privacy — Decent Timer

## App Store Connect → App Privacy answers
The app collects **nothing**: no network calls, no analytics, no accounts, no identifiers.

- "Do you or your third-party partners collect data from this app?" → **No**
- Result: the App Privacy section shows **Data Not Collected**.

That's the whole questionnaire — there are no data types to declare.

## Privacy Policy (REQUIRED URL even for no-data apps)
Apple requires a Privacy Policy URL on the product page. Host the text below at, e.g.,
https://decentespresso.com/privacy and paste that URL into App Store Connect.

---
**Decent Timer — Privacy Policy**

Decent Timer does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. The app runs
entirely on your device. It has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and makes
no network connections. Timer settings are kept only on your device.

Because no data is collected, there is nothing to access, correct, or delete on our servers.

If you have questions, contact: support@decentespresso.com
